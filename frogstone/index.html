Privacy Policy for Frogstone

Last updated: 22 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Frogstone (the “Game”, package name io.github.indiedevpacipaci.frogstone), developed by PaciPaci (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information when you use it.

By downloading or using the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

  • The Game does not require an account and does not ask you for any personal information.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information to serve and measure ads.

Information We Collect

Information the Developer collects directly

None. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. The Game has no login, no registration, and no contact forms.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and preferences — such as your best score, in-game coins, unlocked items, settings, daily-bonus date and tutorial completion — locally on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

Information collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays advertisements using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK and its partners may automatically collect and process certain information, which may include:

  • your device's advertising identifier (e.g. Google Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, and similar technical data),
  • data about ad interactions (impressions and clicks).

This information is collected directly by Google and its advertising partners, not by us. It is used to deliver ads (including, with your consent where required, personalized ads), to measure ad performance, and to prevent fraud and abuse.

For details on how Google uses this data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Consent (EU / EEA / UK)

Where required by law (for example under the GDPR), the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the consent options presented in the Game, and you can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google and its partners under their own privacy policies. We do not share data with any other third parties.

Data Security

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on our own servers, there is no such data for us to secure. Data handled by Google AdMob is protected under Google's security practices.

Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Game after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
