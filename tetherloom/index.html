Privacy Policy for Tetherloom

Developer: PaciPaci · Effective date: 7 May 2026

What we collect

Tetherloom does not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other identifying data.

Local storage

The game saves your progress locally on your device — your best score and completed levels. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tetherloom uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect certain data — such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in AdMob

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), a consent form is shown before personalized ads are served.

Third parties

Aside from Google AdMob, Tetherloom does not use any analytics tools or third-party SDKs that collect data. No personal data is shared with any other external parties.

Children

This game is not directed at children under the age of 13. The game itself does not ask for personal information, and contains no chat and no social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
