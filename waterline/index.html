Privacy Policy for Waterline

Developer: PaciPaci · Effective date: 20 July 2026

This privacy policy describes how the mobile game Waterline (“the app”) handles your information. The short version: the app itself collects no personal data and requires no account. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which serves optional rewarded ads.

Data the app itself collects

None. Your game progress — level, unlocked boats, upgrades, settings and best scores — is stored only locally on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The app does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location, or any other personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Waterline shows rewarded ads only — short videos you can voluntarily watch in exchange for an in-game bonus (an extra life or doubled fireflies). There are no forced banner or interstitial ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, and coarse device diagnostics. This processing is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in AdMob

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are loaded, and you can choose whether personalized ads may be shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It contains no chat, no social features, and no in-app purchases.

Data deletion

All game data lives on your device only — uninstalling the app removes it completely. The developer holds no copy of your data and therefore cannot be asked to delete it; for data processed by Google in connection with advertising, please use the Google links above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example when a feature is added), the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
