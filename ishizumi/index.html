Privacy Policy for Ishizumi

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Ishizumi (the “Game”, package name io.github.indiedevpacipaci.ishizumi), developed by PaciPaci (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information when you use it.

By downloading or using the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

  • The Game does not require an account and does not ask you for any personal information.
  • The Game is offline: there are no leaderboards, no online profiles, and no servers of ours it talks to.
  • Your settings and your unfinished tower are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one (to receive a handful of extra stones), through Google AdMob.

Information We Collect

Information the Developer collects directly

None. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. The Game has no login, no registration, and no contact forms.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves a small settings file on your device containing only:

  • your sound and music switches,
  • whether you have seen the tutorial,
  • how high you have built (used only by the Game itself; no score is ever displayed),
  • the tower you left unfinished, so it is still standing when you come back.

This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

Information collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays advertisements using Google AdMob. It uses a single ad format — a rewarded ad, shown only when you deliberately choose to watch one in exchange for a handful of extra stones. There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play.

To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK and its partners may automatically collect and process certain information, which may include:

  • your device's advertising identifier (e.g. Google Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, and similar technical data),
  • data about ad interactions (impressions and clicks).

This information is collected directly by Google and its advertising partners, not by us. It is used to deliver ads (including, with your consent where required, personalized ads), to measure ad performance, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Ishizumi itself does not have access to this information.

For details on how Google uses this data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Consent (EU / EEA / UK)

Where required by law (for example under the GDPR), the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the “privacy” link on the Game's title screen (shown where the consent form applies), and you can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Your Choices in the United States

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (such as California under the CCPA/CPRA, and other states with comparable laws), you have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising. We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not collect any. To limit how Google and its advertising partners process information for personalized advertising, use the “privacy” link on the Game's title screen — it opens Google's own privacy options, including the “Do not sell or share my personal information” choice where it applies. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google and its partners under their own privacy policies. We do not share data with any other third parties.

Data Security

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on our own servers, there is no such data for us to secure. Data handled by Google AdMob is protected under Google's security practices.

Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). It contains no chat, no social features, and no in-app purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so we can address it.

Your Rights and Choices

  • You can review or withdraw your ads consent at any time through the “privacy” link on the Game's title screen.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the Game at any time to remove all locally stored data.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data (such as access, correction, or deletion). Since we do not hold any personal data about you, such requests are best directed to Google regarding data collected through AdMob (see the links above).
